01 — Introduction

What is SeoulMate?

A digital travel companion designed to make exploring Seoul easier, more personal, and more meaningful for international visitors.

Our Idea

Exploring a new country can be exciting, but language barriers, unfamiliar transportation, and difficulty finding authentic local experiences can make traveling challenging.

SeoulMate brings essential travel services together in one platform. Travelers can discover destinations, plan daily itineraries, connect with local guides, and join group experiences.

Our goal is to help visitors experience Seoul beyond ordinary tourist attractions.

02 — Our Approach

Making Seoul Easier to Explore

We identified common challenges faced by international visitors and designed SeoulMate to address them.

The Challenge

Travelers may struggle with language barriers, unfamiliar transportation systems, disconnected travel information, and finding people to explore the city with.

Independent Planning

Create personalized daily itineraries and organize destinations into practical routes.

Group Experiences

Discover themed tours and meet other travelers through local group activities.

Private Local Guides

Connect with local guides based on language, interests, and neighborhood knowledge.
