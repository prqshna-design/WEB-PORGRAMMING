Labels & Accessibility

Accessibility (a11y) makes websites easier for everyone to use. HTML labels help users understand form controls, support screen readers, and make the associated control easier to select.

Method 1: Explicit Linking

The label's for attribute matches the input's id. Clicking the label focuses the text field.

Method 2: Implicit Wrapping

The checkbox is placed inside its label, so a separate for attribute is not required.
