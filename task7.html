Kimchi Fried Rice

What You Need

  • 2 cups cooked rice
  • 1 cup kimchi
  • 1 tablespoon cooking oil
  • 1 egg
  • 1 tablespoon soy sauce
  • 1 green onion

Steps

  1. Heat the cooking oil in a pan.
  2. Cut the kimchi into small pieces and add it to the pan.
  3. Add the cooked rice and soy sauce, then mix everything together.
  4. Be careful because the pan and oil will be very hot.
  5. Cook the rice for a few minutes until it is well mixed and heated.
  6. For extra flavor, add a fried egg on top before serving.
